## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Will this work on my computer?

This tool works on most Windows computers with Intel or AMD processors. It reads data from built-in temperature sensors that all modern CPUs have.

### Is this safe to use?

Yes. The tool only reads information from your computer — it doesn't change any settings or modify system files. It's a monitoring utility, not a tweaking tool.

### What temperature is too high?

Generally, temperatures above 90°C (194°F) are considered dangerous for most CPUs. For continuous heavy use, staying under 85°C is recommended. Your computer may have its own limits built in — this tool helps you stay ahead of those.

### Why does the icon show different numbers than other tools?

Different programs may read sensors slightly differently. Some show an average, others show the hottest core. This tool shows you the actual per-core readings so you know exactly what's happening.

### Does it work with AMD and Intel?

Yes. Both processor brands are fully supported.

### Can I turn off the sound alert?

Yes. In Settings, uncheck **"Enable Audio Alert"**. You can keep visual monitoring without sound warnings.

## 📋 Troubleshooting

### The icon doesn't show up
- Click the system tray arrow (^) to show hidden icons
- If it's still not there, check your taskbar settings to allow icons for hidden apps
- Try restarting the application

### No temperature readings appear
- Make sure you have the latest drivers for your CPU chipset
- Try running the application as administrator (right-click the file, select "Run as administrator")
- Some older computers may lack proper sensor support

### The sound doesn't play
- Check your system volume — make sure it's not muted
- Verify in Settings that audio alerts are enabled
- Test with your regular sounds to confirm your speakers work

### The CPU shows very high temps
- Check that your computer's fans are spinning properly
- Make sure ventilation is clear — no dust blocking air vents
- Consider reapplying thermal paste if your computer is several years old
- The tool can help you diagnose, but fixing physical cooling issues may require a technician
